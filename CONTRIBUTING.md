# Contributing

**In plain words:** you are welcome to propose a change. It comes in as a pull request from your own copy of this
repository (a fork); a session reviews it and brings it in, and the owner merges it (`AGENTS.md` §3). Before
anything is merged, you agree to the terms below. They keep the owner free to use the project any way he chooses,
selling it included, which the non-commercial licences would otherwise stop him doing with your part.

## The terms

By sending a contribution (code, art, writing, or anything else) you agree that:

1. **You made it, or you have the right to give it on these terms.** It carries nothing you may not share: no one
   else's code or art, no key or password, and no personal detail about anyone.
2. **The owner (rcguerrero29) may use it any way.** You give him a permanent, worldwide, free and irrevocable
   licence to use, copy, change, publish, sublicense and sell your contribution, and to license it under any
   terms he chooses. You keep your copyright: this is a licence, not a transfer.
3. **It is published here under the licence of the part it joins** ([LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md)).
4. **If an AI agent made it, the person who directed the agent agrees on its behalf.**

A pull request is merged only when it says it agrees: tick the box in the pull request template.

## How

- Work from a fork, and sign your commits with a no-reply address (`test/authors.js` checks).
- Run the checks in `AGENTS.md` §4 before you open the pull request.
- A security problem goes to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), never into a pull request or an issue.
