## What that means

- **Anyone may**, for any purpose that is not commercial: play it, read it, copy it, change it and share it.
- **Sharing the art or the writing** needs credit ("Meridian Quest, by rcguerrero29, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0") and the
  same licence on what you share.
- **Nobody else may** sell any of it, or use it to make money.
- **The owner may** use, sell or license his own work in any way he chooses. A licence is permission he gives
  others; it does not limit him.
